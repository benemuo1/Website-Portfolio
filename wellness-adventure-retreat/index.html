The Program
01

Cold Water Immersion

Guided plunges in glacial lakes and rivers — a reset for the nervous system, led by a certified cold-therapy coach.

02

Forest Immersion

Slow, sensory walks rooted in shinrin-yoku practice. No pace, no destination — just the woods.

03

Trail Movement

Sunrise mobility and trail sessions designed to move the body the way it was built to move.

04

Deep Rest

Evenings close with sound rest, unhurried meals, and nights under genuinely dark skies.

What’s Included

Everything is guided. Nothing is rushed.

Every stay includes lodging, meals, and full access to the four pillars of the program — led in small groups of no more than twelve.

    Certified cold-therapy and movement coaches throughout.
