Episode 12 · Winter 2024

The Ones We Keep Coming Back To

Featuring: The God of Small Things · Beloved · Normal People
17:04 Dogeared Podcast 48:22
Show Notes

This week we revisit the books that changed us — the ones with cracked spines, coffee stains, and notes scrawled in the margins. We talk about what it means to reread a book at a different stage of life, and why some stories feel entirely new the second (or fifth) time around.

Joined by two fellow readers, we each bring one book we've returned to again and again, and try to put into words why it still matters.
