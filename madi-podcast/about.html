Meet Madi

Madi is a lifelong reader, reluctant dog-eared page folder, and the voice behind every episode. She started this podcast because she couldn't stop talking about books — and eventually someone told her to just record it.

She reads across genres but has a soft spot for literary fiction, debut novels, and anything that makes her cry on public transport.
