Our Story

A calm space, close to home.

Haven Wellness opened in 2021 with a simple goal: make it easy to fit real rest into a busy week.

Our Approach
01 — Bodywork

Hands-on, always.

Every massage is tailored in the room, not from a fixed menu — your therapist adjusts as you go.

02 — Movement

Yoga for every body.

Small classes, real corrections, and a pace that meets you exactly where you are.

03 — Stillness

Guided, not performative.

Mindfulness sessions built for people who've never meditated a day in their life.

04 — Community

A studio, not a chain.

Six practitioners who know your name, your schedule, and how your week's been going.

How We Started

Built by people who do this work.

Our founder, a massage therapist by training, wanted a studio where guests never felt rushed through a session. Haven is the result — a small, close-knit team who genuinely enjoy this work.
